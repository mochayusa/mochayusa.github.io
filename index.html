About

I am a university lecturer at the University of Bengkulu, Indonesia. I have a passion for teaching and research in the field of computer science, with a focus on software development and data analysis.

Currently, I am pursuing PhD research projects in the field of Machine Learning for Software Engineering. My research interests include software quality, software testing, and the application of machine learning techniques to improve software development processes.
